# Client Onboarding Workflow

**Fictional portfolio practice sample.** This reusable kit demonstrates an onboarding process for an invented creative studio. It does not represent a real client engagement or measured business results.

## Workflow

Trigger: the studio confirms the signed agreement and any required initial payment. The assistant verifies the trigger with the project owner before sending the welcome message.

| Step | Owner | Target | Complete when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Confirm handoff | Project owner | Day 0 | Approved scope, contacts, start date, and agreement location are recorded |
| Create workspace | Assistant | Day 0 | Standard folders exist and access is restricted to approved participants |
| Send welcome email | Assistant | Day 1 | Approved welcome message and intake link are sent |
| Review intake | Assistant | Day 2 | Missing answers are listed; sensitive information is not requested in the form |
| Schedule kickoff | Assistant | Day 2 | Participants accept a time with a stated time zone and meeting link |
| Prepare kickoff brief | Project owner | Before kickoff | Goals, scope, inputs, and decisions needed are documented |
| Share recap | Assistant | Within one working day of kickoff | Owner-approved recap includes decisions, tasks, and open questions |
| Check first-week handoff | Project owner | Day 5 | Access works, first milestones are clear, and unresolved items have owners |

Targets are proposed service timings for the exercise, not promises to a real client.

## Welcome email template

Subject: Welcome, [client name] - your next steps with [studio name]

Hi [client name],

We're looking forward to working with you on [project name]. I will help coordinate the practical details so you know what is happening and what we need from you.

To get started:

1. Complete the intake form at [approved form link] by [agreed date].
2. Add existing brand materials to [approved upload location].
3. Choose a kickoff time from [scheduling link], which displays [time zone].

Our first session will cover your goals, priorities, and the first milestone. Your main project contact is [owner name], and you can reach us through [agreed channel].

Please use the approved secure sharing process for account access. Do not send passwords in email or the intake form.

Best,
[assistant name]

## Intake form outline

1. Primary contact name, role, and preferred contact method.
2. Project goal and the audience the work should serve.
3. What would a successful deliverable look like?
4. Required deliverables and any known dependencies.
5. Important dates and the reason behind each deadline.
6. Existing assets and their approved sharing location.
7. Final approver and typical review availability.
8. Accessibility, brand, or formatting requirements.
9. Questions the client would like covered in the kickoff.

## Kickoff agenda - 30 minutes

| Minutes | Topic | Desired output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 | Introductions and context | Confirm roles and communication preferences |
| 5-12 | Goals and audience | Agree the main purpose and intended users |
| 12-20 | Scope and first milestone | Confirm deliverables, inputs, and review sequence |
| 20-25 | Dependencies and questions | Assign owners to missing inputs |
| 25-30 | Recap and next steps | Restate decisions, actions, and next check-in |

## Suggested folder structure

```text
[Client] / [Project]
  01_Admin
  02_Client_Inputs
  03_Working_Files
  04_Review
  05_Final_Deliverables
```

Keep agreements and financial documents in the approved restricted location. Do not assume every shared project participant should have access to them.

## Handoff check

- Every promised deliverable matches the agreed scope.
- Every open task has an owner and agreed due date.
- Client access has been checked using the correct account and permissions.
- The recap separates decisions from proposals.
- The project owner reviews commitments before the recap is sent.

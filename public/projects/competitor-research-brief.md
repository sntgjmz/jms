# Competitor Research Brief

> **Fictional portfolio sample.** Every company, offer, observation, and source below was created to demonstrate a virtual-assistance research workflow. This is not market advice and does not describe real businesses.

## Request

Prepare a concise view of how six remote-work brands present their offers. Compare the same criteria for every company, keep observations separate from interpretation, and leave a source trail that another person can review.

## Research frame

- **Question:** How do comparable brands explain a remote-work product or service to first-time visitors?
- **Audience:** A fictional small team preparing a positioning workshop.
- **Criteria:** primary audience, headline promise, core offer, lead channel, proof style, and content pattern.
- **Review rule:** Record only what appears in the fictional source notes. Mark an open question rather than filling a gap with an assumption.
- **Scope:** Homepage and one sample content page per company. Pricing, traffic, revenue, and customer sentiment are outside this exercise.

## Structured profiles

### 1. North & Fern

- **Observable sample facts:** The fictional homepage introduces a physical desk collection, followed by three guide cards. Product pages show an item in a work setting and list care instructions.
- **Strength in the sample:** The visual and written language support one calm, practical theme.
- **Possible gap:** The sample does not explain how the individual products work together as a system.
- **Open question:** Do guide visitors return to product pages after reading?

### 2. Common Desk

- **Observable sample facts:** The homepage leads with a weekly planning template and an email signup. A sample workflow screenshot appears before pricing.
- **Strength in the sample:** The visitor sees the structure of the offer early.
- **Possible gap:** The fictional source does not show whether the template fits solo users.
- **Open question:** Which team size receives the clearest value from the template?

### 3. Daymark Studio

- **Observable sample facts:** Social-style visuals lead into a digital planning kit. The page uses short captions and layout comparisons.
- **Strength in the sample:** The offer is easy to scan and visually consistent.
- **Possible gap:** Setup time and ongoing maintenance are not described.
- **Open question:** What support is included after purchase?

### 4. Workwell Co.

- **Observable sample facts:** A long-form article introduces the course. Instructor background and an eight-week outline appear on the offer page.
- **Strength in the sample:** The teaching structure is specific.
- **Possible gap:** The source has limited evidence of what the exercises look like.
- **Open question:** Is there a sample lesson or worksheet?

### 5. Quiet Hours

- **Observable sample facts:** A webinar is the main entry point. The toolkit page includes two policy excerpts and a manager checklist.
- **Strength in the sample:** The problem and intended buyer are clearly named.
- **Possible gap:** The fictional page does not address individual contributor concerns.
- **Open question:** How does the toolkit support team feedback before a policy is adopted?

### 6. Field Note Office

- **Observable sample facts:** The resource library appears before the paid bundle. Each template preview states its owner and use case.
- **Strength in the sample:** Visitors can understand the intended workflow before purchase.
- **Possible gap:** The relationship among the templates is only shown on the bundle page.
- **Open question:** Is there a recommended order for first-time setup?

## Early patterns

1. **Education comes before the offer in four of six profiles.** This is an observation from the fictional sample, not evidence that the pattern causes more sales.
2. **The clearest pages show part of the deliverable.** Screenshots, excerpts, or previews make an unfamiliar system easier to understand.
3. **Audience specificity varies.** Three brands name a narrow role; the others describe a general remote-work problem.
4. **Setup expectations are often missing.** Four profiles leave an open question about time, sequence, or support.

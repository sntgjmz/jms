## Preparation checklist

- Confirm traveler details, required travel documents, and entry requirements using current official sources.
- Obtain approval for proposed costs before making bookings.
- Check meeting locations, local times, accessibility needs, and host contacts.
- Save booking confirmations and required documents in the agreed secure location.
- Add venue addresses, meeting links, and agendas to calendar events.
- Share the final itinerary and emergency contact plan with the traveler.
- Recheck flight status, weather, and transport arrangements close to departure.

## If plans change

1. Confirm the change from the airline, accommodation, or host directly.
2. Identify affected meetings and existing cancellation terms.
3. Present available options and costs to the traveler for a decision.
4. Update the calendar and itinerary after the change is agreed.
5. Inform affected meeting hosts using the agreed communication process.

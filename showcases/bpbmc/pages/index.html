About us

Blessed Paul Bernard Medical Clinic

Founded in 2015, Blessed Paul Bernard Medical Clinic (BPBMC) established its main branch in Barangay Pinagbuhatan, Pasig City with a commitment to deliver exceptional healthcare to our community.

Our Vision

At BPBMC, we envision a healthcare landscape where trust, empathy, and medical excellence form the foundation of every patient's journey.

Our Mission

is to continually provide comprehensive healthcare services, addressing the diverse medical needs of our community.

Join Us in Your Health Journey

We invite you to experience healthcare redefined at Blessed Paul Bernard Medical Clinic. Our doors are open, and our dedicated team is ready to support you in your health journey. With BPBMC, you are not just a patient; you are a valued member of our extended family. Thank you for considering Blessed Paul Bernard Medical Clinic as your trusted healthcare partner.

With Respect and Compassion,
Blessed Paul Bernard Medical Clinic
